# Self- and Peer-Assessment Matrix (Template)

**Coordinator:** Trần Kiện Đông · **Completed:** Week 8 · Each member fills in the form **independently and confidentially** and sends it to the coordinator. Only the aggregated matrix (Section 4) is committed to the repository.

## 1. Criteria

| # | Criterion | What it covers | Evidence to consider |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| C1 | Technical contribution | Quality and quantity of code, data, tests and design work | Merged PRs, reviewed code, artefacts in RACI |
| C2 | Documentation & reports | Contribution to reports, README, docs and slides | Report sections, doc PRs |
| C3 | Reliability & time management | Meets agreed deadlines; keeps the Kanban board current | Action items in minutes, issue history |
| C4 | Communication & collaboration | Attends meetings, responds on time, helps others, gives useful reviews | Attendance, PR reviews |
| C5 | Professionalism & ethics | Honest reporting, respects licences and academic integrity, constructive conduct | Behaviour, review comments |
| C6 | Initiative & problem solving | Identifies risks, proposes solutions, unblocks the team | Minutes, issues raised and closed |

## 2. Scoring guide (per criterion)

| Score | Descriptor |
| :---: | :--- |
| 5 | Excellent: consistently exceeds expectations; a role model for the team |
| 4 | Good: fully meets expectations with minor lapses |
| 3 | Satisfactory: meets the core expectations; occasional reminders needed |
| 2 | Weak: frequently misses expectations; others had to compensate |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory: little or no contribution in this area |

Rules: score yourself as well as each peer. Any score of 1, 2 or 5 needs a one-line justification. Do not give everyone identical scores without reflection.
